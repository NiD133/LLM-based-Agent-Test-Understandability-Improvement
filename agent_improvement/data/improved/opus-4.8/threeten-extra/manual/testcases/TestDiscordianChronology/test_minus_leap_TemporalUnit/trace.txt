                 /**
                  * Each row describes one subtraction:

                  *     amount, unit,                                // the amount and unit to subtract

                  * </pre>
                  * i.e. {@code DiscordianDate.of(base...).minus(amount, unit)} must equal
                  * {@code DiscordianDate.of(expected...)}.
