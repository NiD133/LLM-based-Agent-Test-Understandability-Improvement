                  *   <li>{@code 524286} - the encoded kx value derived from the run length.</li>
                  *   <li>{@code 1} - the canonical specifier for the A sub-codec (BYTE1).</li>
                  *   <li>{@code 1} - the canonical specifier for the B sub-codec (BYTE1).</li>
